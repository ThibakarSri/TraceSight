# Data provenance and publication policy
Dataset: [OpenRCA 2.0 / ops-lite](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anon-ops/ops-lite), historical metadata revision `581eba39d0bfd2d397eded6cd7cbe3303255521a`. Follow the publisher's data-use and attribution terms and institutional requirements. The small audit reused cached files; exact remote-byte correspondence was not reverified. The full audit was reported for the abnormal window; the LR pilot used 90 development-exposed incidents. Reserve genuinely untouched eligible cases for final evaluation. Raw telemetry, case-level feature/prediction tables, exact trace identifiers, answer keys and private paths are not included. Keep these local; no licence for dataset redistribution is implied. Aggregate results are historical research outputs, not a new evaluation. Before publication, verify rights to distribute source and summaries; no open-source code licence is assumed. See the local original archives for exact case IDs and hashes; do not publish those without review.
